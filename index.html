01出国上课

第 1 天目录

Last summer, Mei flew to London for a language course. Aboard the plane, she was excited. But in London, people spoke with a strong accent, and at first it was hard to communicate. One day it rained heavily, and her host said, “It’s raining cats and dogs!” Mei ran to the window to look for the animals. She took the idiom literally! Everyone laughed, but in a kind and polite way. “Don’t worry. That’s a typical mistake,” they said. After a month, Mei had adapted to life in London.

去年夏天，小梅飞到伦敦上语言课。在飞机上，她很兴奋。可到了伦敦，那里的人口音很重，一开始她很难和别人交流。有一天下大雨，房东说：“It's raining cats and dogs!”（意思是“雨下得很大”，字面意思是“天上在下猫和狗”）小梅跑到窗边去找小动物——她把这句习语按字面意思理解了！大家都笑了，不过笑得很友善、很有礼貌。“别担心，这是很典型的错误。”他们说。一个月后，小梅已经适应了伦敦的生活。

  • accent/ˈæksənt/n. 重音；口音
  • communicate/kəˈmjuːnɪkeɪt/vt. 传达，交流，通讯；vi. 传达，传播
  • idiom/ˈɪdiəm/n. 成语，方言，土语；习惯用语
  • literally/ˈlɪtərəli/ad. 照字面意义，逐字地

02网购被骗

Tom was browsing the Internet when he saw an advertisement: “An amazing new phone for only 99 yuan!” The seller made a promise: free and fast delivery. Tom made a hasty decision and paid from his bank account at once. The phone never came. It was a fraud. The “seller” had only pretended to run a shop. Now Tom always stops and thinks before he pays.

汤姆正在上网浏览，看到一条广告：“惊人好价！新款手机只要 99 元！”卖家还许下承诺：包邮，发货快。汤姆仓促做了决定，马上从银行账户付了款。手机一直没到——这是一场骗局。那个“卖家”只是假装在开店。现在汤姆付钱之前，总会先停下来想一想。
